Deleting Your Data

Last updated: August 1, 2026

In short: Sıfırla stores nothing on any server. All of your data lives only on your own device — so deleting it is entirely in your hands. Two scenarios below.

1. If the app is installed: delete from within the app

  1. Open Sıfırla and go to the More tab in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap "Delete all my data".
  3. Read the notice: all your debts, payment history, settings and your name will be permanently deleted from this device; this cannot be undone. If you wish, tap "Back up first" to download a copy.
  4. Confirm with "Yes, delete everything". The app returns to its first-launch state and shows a brief confirmation.

2. If you already uninstalled the app

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of your data; nothing remains on your device. There is nothing to delete on our side either — we never had any of your data on a server.

If you used Sıfırla in a browser, clearing your browser's site data for sifirla.app achieves the same result.

Questions or requests

If you have any question or request about data deletion, write to us: destek@sifirla.app
